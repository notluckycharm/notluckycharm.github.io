Hello!

I'm Jacob, a second year PhD student of Linguistics at NYU and recent graduate from Harvard University (c/o '24) where I received a Joint Bachelors of Arts in Linguistics and Computer Science (Highest Honors), along with a language citation in Modern Japanese. My theoretical research interests include agreement, A/Ā distinction, and locality.

I am also passionate about language revitalization and documentation. I have linguistic fieldwork experience in languages from several language families:

  • Alabama (Muskogean)
  • Georgian (Kartvelian)
  • Japanese
  • Korean
  • Oshiwambo (Bantu)
